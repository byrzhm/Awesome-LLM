# Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

RLHF uses human preferences to learn a reward signal and improve a language model's policy. For a curated reading list covering RLHF, preference optimization, verifiable rewards, reasoning, and agent training, see [Reinforcement Learning for LLMs](reinforcement_learning.md). Distributed implementations are covered separately in [RL training systems](../ai_infra/reinforcement_learning.md).

Starting points:

- [Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) — InstructGPT's supervised fine-tuning, reward modeling, and PPO pipeline.
- [Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) — Critique-based revision and reinforcement learning from AI feedback.
- [Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) — An RL-free preference-learning comparison that avoids an explicit reward-model training stage.
